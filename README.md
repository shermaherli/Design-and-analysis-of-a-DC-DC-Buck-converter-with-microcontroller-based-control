# Automotive Buck Converter

Design and analysis of a DC-DC Buck converter for voltage regulation.
applying power electronics and control theory with microcontroller-based control.

## Problem
A DC power source needed to deliver a stable, lower output voltage 
regardless of load variations, using an efficient switching topology.

## Solution
Designed a DC-DC Buck converter and implemented closed-loop control 
using a microcontroller to regulate the output voltage.

## My Role
- Circuit design and component selection
- Simulation of the converter
- Programming the microcontroller for voltage regulation
- Testing and tuning of the control loop

## Tools and Technologies
- **Hardware:** Microcontroller, power electronics components (MOSFET, inductor, capacitor, diode)
- **Software:** MATLAB y Proteus
- **Concepts:** Power electronics, DC-DC conversion, control theory, transfer functions

## Key Process
1. Theoretical design of the Buck converter (duty cycle, inductor, capacitor).
2. Simulation to validate the design under different load conditions.
3. Implementation of the control algorithm on the microcontroller.
4. Experimental testing and adjustment of the control loop.

## Results
- Regulated output voltage under load variations.
- Validated the design through simulation and experimental tests.
- Integrated power electronics with digital control.

## What I Learned
- How to design and tune a DC-DC converter.
- Integration of hardware and firmware for control applications.
- Practical application of control theory in power electronics.
